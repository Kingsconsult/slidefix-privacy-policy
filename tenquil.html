Privacy Policy

Tenquil: Number Match Puzzle
Effective Date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tenquil: Number Match Puzzle ("we", "us", "the App", package com.kings.tenquil) handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: Tenquil is an offline number match puzzle game. The App has no internet access, so it cannot send any data to us or anyone else. It does not require an account, does not show ads, has no real-money purchases, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not sell your data. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

None. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number, photos, or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no chat, and no leaderboards.

B. Information Stored on Your Device

To save your progress, the App keeps a small amount of data in its private storage on your device:

  • Game Progress: current stage, stages cleared, best score, Daily Challenge days completed, and the board you are currently playing.
  • In-Game Items: virtual coins, boosters (extra adds and hints), owned themes, and daily-reward streaks. These have no cash value.
  • App Settings: sound, music, volumes, haptics, reduced motion, selected theme, and whether you finished the tutorial.

This data is never transmitted. We cannot see it.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports, usage analytics, or browsing history

3. Data Storage & Security

All App data is stored in Android's private app storage. It is accessible only to the App and is protected by the Android operating system's sandboxing. We do not operate any server that receives data from the App.

If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include the App's saved progress in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we have no access to it.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, advertising networks, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools.

5. Permissions

The App requests no special Android permissions. It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

Motion Sensor

While a tiled theme is on screen, the App reads your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) so the tiles catch the light as you tilt your phone. This needs no permission. The readings are used on your device in real time and are never stored or sent anywhere.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your information with anyone. Because the App has no internet access, no data leaves your device.

7. Data Retention

Your game data stays on your device until you reset it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. We retain nothing, because we receive nothing.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset progress in the App,
  • Clearing the App's data in your device Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the App adds new features or services. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where the law requires it, we will also ask for your consent in the App. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
